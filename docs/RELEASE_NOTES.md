Download **AssistiveVision-1.0-debug.apk** from the files below and open it on your Android phone. No ZIP extraction or build tools are required.

- Android 8.0 or later, arm64; x86_64 is also included for emulators.
- Spanish and English speech, voices and detector included; app use works offline.
- Research prototype signed with an Android debug certificate. A build signed with another key may require preserving app data before installing it separately.

The attached `SHA256SUMS`, `build-manifest.json`, signature/metadata reports, `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY.md` identify the exact APK, source commit and bundled assets. Rebuilding from source can produce a different signature and APK bytes.
